FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hexa Innovative.

Is this desktop software or an online tool?
+
Hexa Innovative is a desktop-based Windows software. It processes data locally on your PC for better speed and privacy, while internet is used only for live data extraction.
What data can it extract?
+
15+ fields: business name, phone number, website, email, full address, GPS coordinates, star rating, review count, business category, and working hours — everything needed for precision outreach.
Can it bypass the 120-result limit?
+
Yes. Our Deep-Scan Engine segments searches by sub-areas and categories, extracting thousands of results per location — far beyond the standard 120-result cap Google Maps imposes.
Is CSV export included?
+
Absolutely — all plans include one-click CSV export. Files are clean, structured, and compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, WhatsApp bulk senders, CRM tools, and email platforms.
Is it beginner friendly?
+
Completely. Install, enter search criteria, hit extract — most users get their first leads in under 5 minutes. Full video tutorials are included with every plan.
Can agencies use this?
+
Yes — the Agency Plan includes 10+ PC licenses, team access, unlimited projects, and an agency dashboard. Many agencies run Hexa for dozens of clients simultaneously.
Does it support multiple projects?
+
Yes. Pro and Agency plans support unlimited simultaneous campaigns organized by city, niche, or client within the Multi-Project Workspace — perfect for scaling agencies.
